# What's new

## 1.2.0 — 14-11-2026

- A living logo with the gold ball on the left bar's home button, and a living brain on Copilot's tile.
- Copilot's microphone listens as long as you speak — "Send" sends the question, "Done" puts it in the box to check first.
- Copilot understands "all stock", "today's stock", "what stock is there" — the largest quantities first, with a count of what has run out.
- Copilot knows products by meaning — it finds the right one even with a small spelling slip.
- The field-force tree: who is under whom, which position, which depots and which areas — on one page.
- "Rows per page" under every list — 25, 50, 100 or 200; the choice is remembered on every list.

## 1.1.0 — 13-11-2026

- A living brain beside search on the top bar — tap it to ask Copilot full screen, by typing or by voice; it reads the answer aloud.
- Copilot's microphone now asks for permission — tap "Allow" once.
- Copilot for everyone who holds its key.
- The approval screen shows a purchase bill's lines — product, quantity, free, rate, total.
- Field-force safety: inside the dealer wall no depot means nothing; targets and commission only for one's own team.
- Bengali names for every new permission and setting on the control panel.

## 1.0.0 — 24-10-2026

- ABOS now carries a version: the bottom bar shows the version, the date and the running code.
- This page lists what each version brought.
