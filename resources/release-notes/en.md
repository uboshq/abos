# What's new

## 1.3.6 — 19-11-2026

- Home: the head on one line; "Top 10 customers" (was "shops"); top products, customers and collection by channel fall back to the month or year when today is empty; channel collection now counts voucher receipts too.
- The notice bar at the foot is never blank: with nothing urgent it says "No urgent notice today, all is well".
- "ABOS" top bar: the module ⊞ at the far left, the live logo in a white box sized to it; ⊞ opens a centred popup.
- Page heads on one row: name ⓘ · tabs · filter and chips … buttons. No description line under titles, hover ⓘ instead. More than five tabs fold into one slim "▾" button.
- List heads in one white rounded box, stronger row stripes; the coloured cards above a list at a third of their height, on one line.
- In "ABOS", hiding the left menu brings the current module's menu across the top.
- "Dynamic": right-hand module menus no longer run off the screen; company name bold in the bar with the branch below; your picture at the far right; description lines behind ⓘ.
- "SAP": the module bar on one line, no scrolling.

## 1.3.5 — 19-11-2026

- New look "ABOS", now everyone's look: a flat blue menu, a module picker at its foot (arrange it your way), a thin round footer with the running notices. Anyone can go back to their earlier look on the Appearance page.
- The "ABOS" home is a whole-business board: 8 colourful charts first, then colourful number tiles and the gold "cash and bank" box, principal, commission and point lists, every company in a row, and today's to-dos.
- The "SAP" look now follows this year's SAP (Horizon): a white bar, space tabs, white tiles, a floating save bar.
- The "Dynamic" look now follows Business Central: a black bar, menus across the top, dense lists, forms in sections.
- The Appearance page in a new order: ABOS, ABOS Navy, ABOS Blue, Dynamic; then SAP, Odoo, NetSuite, Redwood; then Salesforce, Linear, Classic.
- The day-close popup's "never show" works; the Day Book follows the branch picked in the header; the deposit register has its menu icon.

## 1.3.4 — 19-11-2026

- New look "ABOS Blue": a bright blue frame, round floating panels, light and dark; now everyone's default. Go back to ABOS Navy or any other look in one click on the Appearance page, now in a new order, four to a row.
- The home page opens on its charts; the number tiles, the cash and bank box and "To do today" sit below, each to-do with its word: urgent, today, this week.
- Day Book: one day's journals, entry by entry, with a print.
- Day Closing: money in and out, deliveries, sales and margin, expenses, the cash count, balances, profit and loss; close the day, reopen it with a reason. Cash held by staff shows in a yellow box, by name.
- Day-close checklist: cash count, vehicle fares, drafts, approvals, deposits not yet matched in the bank, each with a link. An open working day comes back the next morning as a reminder in the middle of the screen; it can be closed and never stops work.
- At 5 pm, whoever still holds company cash is told.
- Month Close; trial balance by class and a balance sheet with notes and ratios: the full statement set.
- Deposit reports: matched against the bank statement, a money receipt, a daily deposit register.

## 1.3.3 — 17-11-2026

- Every list and report prints on the ledger paper — "Print view" opens in the browser, "PDF file" downloads; one info line on top, the action column never reaches paper.
- Date buttons on the list bar — today, yesterday, 7 days, this month, last month, 90 days, or your own; the row action is now a small ⋯, its name only in the column head.
- Trial balance in 6 columns — opening, movement and closing, each debit and credit; a figure opens that account's ledger.
- Balance sheet print in the IAS 1 layout, beside it the last day of the previous financial year.
- Deposit slips: gallery and WhatsApp photos work; picking a photo opens the scanner — finds the corners, straightens, clean/original colour/grey/black-and-white; under 100 KB (a setting); the same slip coming again is recognised.
- The approval "Sure?" box names whose paper it is — now above the question.
