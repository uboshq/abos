## Standards to follow
- **IAS 16** Property, Plant and Equipment: recognition at cost (purchase price + directly attributable costs such as freight, installation, duties), components, subsequent costs (capitalise vs expense), depreciation methods (straight-line, diminishing/reducing balance, units of production), useful life and residual value reviewed at least yearly (change in estimate → prospective), derecognition with gain/loss to P&L.
- **IAS 36** impairment (record an impairment loss on a test the owner enters; no automatic valuation).
- **Bangladesh tax depreciation** is different from book depreciation: keep a separate tax-depreciation schedule per asset class with the rates the owner sets (no hard-coded legal rates; owner configures them), and a report comparing book vs tax.
- Capitalisation threshold: items below an owner-set amount go to expense, not the register (control-panel setting).
